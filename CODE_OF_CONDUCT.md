# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, empathetic, and focused on what is best for the community. Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this code. Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project maintainers through the repository's available private contact mechanism.

This text is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
